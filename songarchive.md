## 2. Rules for maintaining this archive

1. Preserve the original song title wherever it is known.
2. Do not silently rename songs to improve spelling, grammar, SEO or marketing appeal.
3. Record spelling variations and alternate titles as aliases until the canonical title can be confirmed.
4. Do not count duplicate WAV and MP3 files as separate songs.
5. Do not count instrumental, vocal, drum, bass, guitar or other stems as independent finished songs.
6. Record remixes and alternate mixes as versions linked to their parent song when that relationship is supported.
7. Keep mashups identifiable as mashups; do not assume they are separate official streaming releases.
8. Do not invent streaming URLs, release dates, ISRCs, UPCs, track numbers, artist credits or licensing details.
9. Preserve explicit credits. Suzuki is a media guru and band manager and performs backing vocals on selected songs; do not automatically credit her as lead vocalist on every track.
10. Keep the catalogue usable by the music, character, SEO and social content agents.
11. When an entry is uncertain, label it for verification instead of inventing an answer.
12. Never downgrade the confirmed licensing status of the master files based solely on missing metadata.

## 8. Duplicate and alias policy

When two filenames look similar:

1. Compare the actual audio or reliable metadata if available.
2. Determine whether they are duplicates, alternate formats, alternate mixes, or genuinely different recordings.
3. Preserve both original filenames in the archive.
4. Link duplicate formats to the same song/version record.
5. Record alternate titles as aliases unless an official title can be established.
6. Keep genuinely different mixes separate under the parent song.
7. Do not delete a catalogue entry merely because a similar title exists.
